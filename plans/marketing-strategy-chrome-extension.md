## Finding Small YouTubers for Outreach

**Search YouTube for:**
- "Chrome extension review"
- "productivity tools for creators"
- "YouTube tools review"

**Filter:** Upload date last month, channels 5K-50K subscribers.

**Other sources:**
- r/NewTubers, r/PartneredYouTube
- VidIQ/TubeBuddy comment sections

## LinkedIn & Twitter Audiences

**LinkedIn:**
1. Enable Creator Mode
2. List 50 larger accounts (5K-100K followers) in creator/YouTube tools space
3. Engage daily on their posts
4. Use Collaborative Articles for "Top Voice" badges
5. Hashtags: #CreatorEconomy, #YouTubeCreators, #ContentCreator

**Twitter/X:**
- Same engagement strategy
- Post GIFs made with tool as content marketing
- Follow accounts that VidIQ/TubeBuddy engage with
